Growth comes from deepening self-awareness and healing through connection. I create a steady, safe space where clients can explore their internal patterns, process difficult experiences, and express themselves authentically.

My Approach

How I work

I do this work because I've seen what changes when people actually understand themselves, not just learn ways to cope. Many of the people I work with have self-awareness but still feel stuck, and that usually means something deeper is going on. I care about getting to that deeper level, especially when trauma or old patterns are part of the picture, and helping them make sense of it in a way that actually creates change.

My approach is holistic and parts-informed, grounded in the belief that real healing involves the full spectrum of who we are. I work primarily with individuals navigating trauma, identity shifts, relationship wounds, and life transitions. Sessions are conversational in nature. I provide space for you to deepen your emotional and personal narrative, not just manage symptoms.

I integrate EMDR through both a parts-informed and attachment-informed lens, drawing from Internal Family Systems to work with the different parts of a person's experience. Often the deepest wounds are relational, the beliefs we formed early about whether we are safe, lovable, or enough. This work helps you find your own inner capacity to heal those wounds, not just manage around them.

I have completed training in Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy (KAP) and provide KAP in collaboration with a medical prescriber. For some clients, KAP offers a way to go even deeper into that work. When someone is ready and it feels like the right fit, KAP can help access parts of the experience that are harder to reach through talk therapy alone.

I value continued learning and believe that therapists should stay curious, humble, and engaged in their own growth.

"The curious paradox is that when I accept myself just as I am, then I can change." — Carl Rogers

The right therapist isn’t the same for everyone. Finding someone who feels like a good fit matters. The relationship between client and therapist is the foundation everything else is built on. Without it, even the best techniques fall flat. I work really well with the right clients, and I'd rather help you find your fit than have you settle for one that isn't quite right. Feel free to reach out to explore whether working together makes sense for you.

Training & Background
